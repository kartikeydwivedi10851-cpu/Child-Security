Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 2026

Relaya is committed to creating a safe environment for all users and maintaining zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. This document outlines our standards and practices to prevent, detect, and respond to CSAE content and behaviour.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Relaya strictly prohibits any content, behaviour, or activity that sexually exploits or endangers minors. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors
  • Sexual conversations involving or directed at minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or requesting any material that sexualises minors

Any user found to be engaging in such behaviour will be immediately and permanently banned, and we will report the incident to the appropriate authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

2. Age Requirements

Relaya requires all users to be at least 13 years of age to create an account. Users who indicate they are under 13 are denied access. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

3. Detection and Prevention

We employ multiple layers of protection to detect and prevent CSAE:

  • Content moderation: All user-generated content and voice rooms are subject to our community guidelines and moderation processes.
  • Reporting tools: All users have access to easy-to-use in-app reporting mechanisms for flagging inappropriate content or behaviour.
  • Account review: Accounts reported for CSAE violations are immediately reviewed and suspended pending investigation.
  • Proactive monitoring: We actively monitor platform activity for potential CSAE indicators.

4. Reporting

Users can report suspected CSAE violations directly through the Relaya app or by contacting us at:

Email: safety@relaya.in

We also report any confirmed CSAM to NCMEC's CyberTipline at missingkids.org and cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies.

5. User Education

Relaya provides resources and guidance within the app to help users — especially younger users — understand online safety, recognise inappropriate behaviour, and use available reporting tools.

6. Employee and Moderator Training

All Relaya team members and moderators involved in content review are trained to identify CSAE indicators and follow proper reporting protocols in accordance with applicable laws.

7. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Relaya will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies in investigations involving CSAE. We preserve records and respond to lawful requests in a timely manner.
